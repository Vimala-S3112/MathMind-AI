📌 Performance Metrics

MAE: Mean Absolute Error measures average prediction error.

MSE: Mean Squared Error measures squared prediction error.

RMSE: Root Mean Squared Error is the square root of MSE.

R² Score: Measures how well the model explains the data.

Note: The values shown above are sample demonstration values, not results from trained models.
